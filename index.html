01 · About

A bit about who I am.

I started at SMK Negeri 4 Padalarang — a vocational school. Not the conventional tech path, but that's where I first got real engineering experience, and I never looked back.

Since joining PT eBdesk Teknologi in 2023, I've grown from intern to Team Lead — designing and scaling data pipelines that handle massive volumes of data from web platforms, APIs, and social media. Python, Kafka, Redis, Elasticsearch every day.

Currently pursuing Computer Science at Universitas Pelita Harapan, focusing on Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity. I believe the future of data infrastructure lives at that intersection.

Outside pipelines — I build games in Java, explore systems in Rust and C++, and try to be a signal for the next generation of engineers coming from vocational schools like mine.
